Methodology and limitations

Interpret the results as exploratory computational evidence from public microarray datasets.

No diagnostic use

Pipeline

Sample metadata were matched by GSM. Technical replicate pairs were verified and averaged probe-by-probe for the derived patient-level matrix. Welch independent-sample tests and empirical-Bayes limma fits use existing processed expression; no additional normalization or log transformation was performed.

Limitations

No laboratory, prospective clinical, treatment-response or diagnostic validation. Expression association does not establish causation, receptor status or clinical utility.
